About

I'm a backend engineer who likes the unglamorous parts of software: clean API contracts, sensible data models, and services that behave the same at 3am as they do in the demo.

I started out at Cuesoft, where I initiated and led Cueprise, an ERP platform, heading a 10-person team of designers and developers — and, since there was no sales team, selling it door-to-door too. Since then I've built backend services for a US K-12 safety platform, a geospatial product and a healthtech company.

I also hold an MSc in Data Science for Business (University of Stirling), so I'm comfortable on both sides of the pipeline — building the APIs and making sense of the data they produce. I'm currently doing an MBA at the University of Law, Birmingham.
